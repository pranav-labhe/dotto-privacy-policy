Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Welcome to Dotto. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our mobile game. At Dotto, we value your privacy and aim to be transparent about the data we handle.

Privacy at a Glance: Dotto is primarily an offline-first game. We do not require account creation, we do not store your data on our own servers, and the core game logic runs entirely on your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

Personal Data

Dotto does not collect "Personal Data" in the traditional sense. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Game Progress and Local Data

To provide a seamless experience, Dotto stores the following information locally on your device using a Room database:

  • Player Identity: A custom name you choose in the setup screen.
  • Game Progress: Your current level, total score, and highest level reached.
  • Session State: A history of moves in your current game session, allowing you to resume if the app is closed.
  • Preferences: Toggles for sound effects and haptic feedback.

This data is stored securely in your app's private storage and is not transmitted to us.

2. Third-Party Services

Dotto utilizes third-party SDKs to provide advertising and maintain app performance. These services may collect information automatically.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

We use AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Advertising IDs (AAID).
  • Device information (model, OS version).
  • Approximate location (IP-based).
  • Ad interaction data (clicks and impressions).

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we use Google's Consent Management Platform to ask for your permission before serving personalized ads.

Google Play Services

Dotto relies on Google Play Services for core functionality and SDK support. Google Play Services may collect diagnostic info and usage data for its own purposes.

You can review Google's privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy.

3. Device Permissions

Dotto requests the following permissions to enhance gameplay:

  • VIBRATE Used to provide haptic feedback when you score a point or make a move. You can disable this in the app's Setup screen.
  • INTERNET Used exclusively to fetch and display ads from AdMob.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used by the ad SDK to check if a connection is available.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Dotto is a family-friendly game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the relevant age in your jurisdiction).

We instruct the AdMob SDK to treat ad requests as **child-directed** where required by law or indicated by the user's account settings, which restricts data collection for those users.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your game data is stored locally, you have full control over it:

  • Manual Deletion: You can clear your scores and progress at any time by going to your Android Device Settings > Apps > Dotto > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored progress and move history.

6. Security

We utilize the standard security features of the Android operating system to protect your local data. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Regional Rights

GDPR (European Users)

If you are a resident of the EEA, you have the right to access the personal data we hold (which is limited to the local data on your phone) and to ask for your personal data to be deleted or corrected.

CCPA (California Users)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. Dotto does not "sell" personal information as defined by the CCPA.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
